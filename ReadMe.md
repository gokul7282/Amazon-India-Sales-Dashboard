Amazon India Sales Dashboard

📊 Project Overview

The **Amazon India Sales Dashboard** is an interactive Excel-based business analytics project designed to provide a clear overview of sales, profit, product performance, order status, payment methods, fulfillment methods, and geographic performance.

The dashboard is designed for a **non-technical business manager** to quickly understand business performance and identify important trends and areas that require attention.



🎯 Business Objective

The main objective of this project is to build a simple and easy-to-understand management dashboard that helps business stakeholders:

- Monitor overall sales and profitability
- Track monthly sales and profit trends
- Identify high-performing categories and products
- Analyze delivered, shipped, returned, and cancelled orders
- Understand return and cancellation rates
- Analyze payment method performance
- Compare fulfillment methods
- Identify important states based on sales, profit, and orders



🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- Microsoft Excel
- Pivot Tables
- Pivot Charts
- Excel Formulas
- Data Aggregation
- KPI Analysis
- Business Data Analysis
- Dashboard Design

📌 Key KPIs

The dashboard includes the following key performance indicators:

- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Total Orders
- Total Units Sold
- Average Order Value (AOV)
- Profit Margin %
- Return Rate %
- Cancellation Rate %

📈 Dashboard Sections

1. Overall Sales & Profit Performance

This section provides an overview of the overall business performance.

**Includes:**

- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Total Orders
- Total Units Sold
- Average Order Value
- Profit Margin %
- Monthly Sales & Profit Trend

**Business Question:**

> How is the business performing overall?

2. Category & Product Performance

This section analyzes category-level and product-level performance.

**Includes:**

- Category-wise Sales
- Category-wise Profit
- Category-wise Units Sold
- Top 10 Products by Sales

**Business Question:**

> Which categories and products are driving the business?

 3. Order Status & Revenue Loss

This section provides visibility into customer order outcomes.

**Includes:**

- Delivered Orders
- Shipped Orders
- Returned Orders
- Cancelled Orders
- Return Rate %
- Cancellation Rate %
- Category-wise Returns
- Category-wise Cancellations

**Business Question:**

> How many orders are successfully completed and how many are being lost through returns and cancellations?

 4. Payment, Fulfillment & Geographic Performance

This section analyzes different business channels and geographic performance.

**Includes:**

 Payment Performance
- Sales by Payment Method
- Profit by Payment Method

Fulfillment Performance
- Sales by Fulfillment Method
- Profit by Fulfillment Method

#### Geographic Performance
- State-wise Sales
- State-wise Profit
- State-wise Orders

**Business Question:**

 Which payment methods, fulfillment methods, and states are generating business?
